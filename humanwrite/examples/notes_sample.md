# Notes from "The Glass Castle" (Jeannette Walls)

## Family background
- Jeannette, Brian, Lori, and Ken live with their parents Rose Mary and
  Dax (Dwight Walls).
- The family keeps moving: Oklahoma, New Mexico, Las Vegas, New York.
- Dax is a scientist/chemist type who wants to drill for gold in the desert.
- Rose Mary is an artist, drinks heavily, and leaves the family at times.

## Key quotes (paraphrase or quote short)
- p. 96: Dax "was a man with a plan" — the family moves to Las Vegas to
  find gold.
- p. 121: winter without heat — the kids sleep in a "cold that was almost
  alive"; Jeannette writes about the cold as if it has a body.
- p. 34: Jeannette is left alone in a window at night ("window child")
  while her father is out drinking; she does not tell anyone.
- p. 155: Brian goes missing for years; the family's poverty and chaos
  continue.
- p. 172: "I was not just a kid who was hungry; I was a kid who was
  starving" — paraphrase carefully, the exact wording is close.

## Themes
- Abandonment vs. freedom: the kids are abandoned but also given
  extraordinary independence.
- Hunger as a constant background condition, not a single event.
- The parents' neglect is real, but the children's resilience is the point
  of the book.
- Jeannette becomes a journalist; Brian becomes a detective.

## My own examples (from my life, keep these in the essay)

- My mom calling teachers when I got a C.
- The spelling bee in fifth grade — the whole cafeteria found out.
- My cousin's science fair redo.
- My mom's own teacher who "failed her on purpose."
