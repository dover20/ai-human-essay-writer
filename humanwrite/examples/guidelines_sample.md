# Writing Guidelines (English 111)

- One idea per paragraph. Start with a topic sentence, develop it with
  examples, end with a sentence that ties it back to your argument.
- Quote sparingly: one or two short quotes per section, never a full
  sentence of source text more than once.
- Your opinion is the point of the essay. First person is allowed and
  expected here ("I think", "I believe"). Do not hide behind "some people
  say" without saying what YOU think.
- Vary sentence length. A page of same-length sentences reads flat.
- Avoid "In conclusion" — just end. Your last paragraph should feel like
  the end of your thought, not a summary of it.
- Contractions are fine and normal in this course.
- Cite the book every time you use an idea or a quote from it, with a page
  number.
